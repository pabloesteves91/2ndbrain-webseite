2nd Brain Plus

Plus unlocks sync on all devices and the weekly review. All other features stay free. Plus is available as a monthly subscription, a yearly subscription (with 14 days free the first time in the App Store) or a one-time “Lifetime” purchase. You’ll see the prices in the app before buying.

  • Payment is made through your Apple ID or Google account. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.
  • You can cancel any time: on iPhone/iPad in Settings under your name → Subscriptions; on Android in Google Play → profile picture → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com) or Google Play (order history).
  • If a subscription ends, sync pauses. Your notes stay fully on your device.
