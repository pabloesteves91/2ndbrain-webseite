Privacy

2nd Brain · As of November 2026

In short

Your notes belong to you. There are no ads, no tracking and no analysis of your behaviour.

Without an account

All notes and images are stored only on your device. Nothing leaves your device – except feedback you send yourself.

With cloud sync

If you sign in (email, Apple or Google), your notes and images are stored in Google Firebase (Firestore and Cloud Storage) so they’re available on your devices. Stored are:

  • your email address or the identifier of your Apple/Google account,
  • the text of your notes, attached images and their date, category and status.

Only you can access your data. It is not sold, not analysed and not shared with third parties (except Google as the technical provider for storage).

Feedback

If you send something via Settings → Help → “Send feedback”, your text, the time, the app version and the platform (iPhone/iPad or Mac) are stored in Google Firestore – plus your account identifier if you’re signed in. Only I read the feedback, to improve the app. It is not shared.

Purchases

Purchases of 2nd Brain Plus are handled entirely by Apple. We receive no payment data, only whether Plus is active.

Deleting

In the app, Settings → Cloud sync → “Delete account” removes your account and all data in the cloud. “Delete everything” removes the data on the device.
