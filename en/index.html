Does 2nd Brain cost anything?

The essentials are free, without ads. Sync and the weekly review are part of Plus: CHF 1 a month, CHF 9 a year (14 days free) or CHF 24 once. See Pricing.

I’ve been using 2nd Brain for a while – do I have to pay now?

No. If you used 2nd Brain before Plus, you get Plus free forever. The app recognises this automatically: from the notes on your device or from your sync account. If it ever doesn’t work, e.g. after reinstalling without an account, write to me – I’ll unlock it for you.

Where are my notes stored?

Without an account only on your device. With cloud sync they are also transferred encrypted to Google Firebase, so they’re available on all your devices. Only you have access. More in the privacy policy.

How do I sync iPhone, iPad and Mac?

In the app, go to Settings → Cloud sync and sign in with Apple, Google or email – on every device with the same account.

How do I delete my account and data?

Settings → Cloud sync → “Delete account” removes the account and all data in the cloud. “Delete everything” under Data removes the notes on the device.

A shared link doesn’t open the app?

Without the app installed, the link shows the note in the browser. If 2nd Brain is installed, tap “Open in 2nd Brain”.
